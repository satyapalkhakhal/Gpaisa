## 2. Regressions and still-open defects (live evidence)

### A. NEW REGRESSION — Cloudflare Email Obfuscation creates a sitewide broken link
- **What happens:** every page's footer `mailto:contact@gpaisa.in` (`components/Footer.tsx:25`) is rewritten by Cloudflare into `<a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#…">[email protected]</a>`.
- **Effect on crawlers:** `/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection` returns **404**, and it is linked from all 344 crawled pages (`PAGE-4XX`/`LINK-BROKEN@other`, the only High issues left).
- **E-E-A-T effect:** Google, AI crawlers and anyone without JS see `[email protected]` instead of the contact address on `/contact` and in every footer. For a YMYL site, a visible contact email is a basic trust signal.
- **Fix (off-site):** Cloudflare → Security → Settings (Scrape Shield) → turn **Email Address Obfuscation** off. Alternatively, wrap the address in `<!--email_off-->…<!--/email_off-->`. No code change is needed for the dashboard route.

### B. YMYL data accuracy — `priority-jewels-ipo` is indexable, in the sitemap, and contradicts itself and the public record
This is the sixth record from the 2026-08-30 seed batch. `db/oneoff/2026-10-21-unpublish-sample-investment-records.sql` deliberately excluded it "to verify against the RHP". It passes `isIpoIndexable()` only because it has a published review (`lib/ipoApi.ts:260`).

|---|---|---|
| Total issue size | ₹112 Cr | ₹91.50 Cr fresh issue (the page's own review also says ₹91.50 Cr) |
| Subscription, badge **"Official … final"** | 1.93x overall | ≈100.45x reported |
| P/E (computed) | 58.92x, EPS ₹3.39 | The page's review says "roughly 13.9x post-issue FY26 earnings" |
| Peer table, own row | `3.394230769230769`, `58.92351274787536` … | Unrounded floats (`components/ipo/PeerComparisonTable.tsx:11` uses `n.toString()`) |

- Price band, lot size and listing price (₹230) are correct, so this is partly real and partly invented.
- **Why it matters:** wrong figures under an "Official" badge on a page in the sitemap are exactly what YMYL quality raters are told to treat as low trust. One such page is a sample of the site's reliability.
- **Fix:** set it to draft (or correct every figure from the RHP/NSE and drop the stale verdict).

Related formatting bug: `components/ipo/GmpDailyTable.tsx:43` prints `▼ 0.2999999999999998` on `/ipo/zetwerk-ipo`, an indexable page.

### C. /about still overclaims (MAN-07/MAN-08 partly open)
- **The page still says:** "Your trusted source for … investment guidance" (`app/about/page.tsx:66`), "Expert Financial Analysis … expert commentary" (`:95-97`), "We verify all data from reliable sources" (`:128`) and "✓ Expert Insights … actionable investment advice from industry experts" (`:217-219`). Its metadata calls the site "India's leading financial information portal" (`:11`, `:50`).
- **Two sections down, the same page says:** the site is run by one person, a developer by profession, who "is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser, and nothing on gpaisa.in is investment advice."
- **`/contact` adds more:** market data is "updated in real-time throughout trading hours", against "cached for about a minute" and "indicative" on /about. It also lists "Visit Us … Mumbai" with no address.
- **`/disclaimer` is stale:** it still lists "Agricultural market prices", a section that now returns 410.
- **Why it matters:** contradictory trust claims on the trust pages are a direct E-E-A-T negative.

### D. Hindi pages serve `<html lang="en">` (28 of 28 `/hi` sitemap URLs)
- The root layout hard-codes `lang="en"` (`app/layout.tsx:73`). The Hindi value is only set client-side (`lib/i18n/LanguageContext.tsx:24`).
- Google detects language mainly from content and hreflang, so this is not an indexing blocker. It is still a wrong signal on every Hindi URL and affects accessibility and AI/LLM parsers.
- **Fix:** give `/hi` its own root layout (route groups `app/(en)/layout.tsx` and `app/(hi)/hi/layout.tsx`, each with its own `<html lang>`). The other option is middleware that sets a header read by the root layout.

### E. hreflang not reciprocal: `/hi/category/credit-cards` ↔ `/credit-cards`
- `/hi/category/credit-cards` declares `en-IN` = `/credit-cards`, but `/credit-cards` declares no alternates (`app/credit-cards/page.tsx:38`: `alternates: { canonical }` only).
- The sitemap entry for the Hindi page has no `xhtml:link` either, so page and sitemap disagree.
- Google ignores unreciprocated pairs.
- **Fix:** add `languages: categoryLanguageAlternates('credit-cards', …)` to `/credit-cards`, and emit the pair in `app/sitemap.ts`.

### F. robots.txt at the edge is stale, and Cloudflare Managed robots.txt is still on
- **Stale copy:** `next.config.mjs:66-70` gives `/robots.txt` `max-age=604800`, and Cloudflare caches it for 7 days. Purge `/robots.txt` after every change, or lower the TTL to ~1 h.
- **Managed robots.txt still on:** commit 422e4da says the code "replaces Cloudflare's managed robots.txt", but the dashboard toggle is still on. Two `User-agent: *` groups are therefore served, and Google merges them, so there is no harm to Search.
- **Fix:** Cloudflare → AI Crawl Control / Bots → turn **Managed robots.txt** off, then purge.

### G. Still open from earlier rounds (lower impact)
- **Noindex pages linked from many pages:**
  - 10 noindex silver cities, each linked from 18 pages;
  - `/ipo/core4-engineer-ipo` and `/ipo/shellz-india-ipo`, each linked from 84 pages via the "Other IPOs" widget;
  - `/ipo-calculator/lot-size-calculator`, linked from 14 pages.
- **The widget leaks crawl budget into noindex pages.** Prefer linking indexable IPOs in sitewide widgets.
- **Thin indexable hubs:** `/credit-cards` (202 words), `/ipo/gmp` (113), `/ipo/listing-performance` (133), `/commodities` (175), `/gold-affordability-index` (172).
- **`/about`, `/contact`, `/terms`, `/privacy-policy`, `/disclaimer` and `/sitemap-page` are not in the sitemap.** They are linked from every page, so this is minor.
- **Duplicate robots metas on 16 URLs:** the root layout `index, follow` plus the page's own value. Both say index, so this is harmless but untidy.
- **Gold/silver city pages:**
  - their `Article` JSON-LD has no `image` (19 URLs);
  - their H1 says "Today — 26 October 2026" while the data block says "Rates as of 25 Oct 2026" (checked on origin with a cache-buster, so this is feed lag, not CDN caching).
- **Other low items:**
  - 2 articles have `headline` > 110 chars in JSON-LD;
  - `/articles/why-silver-prices-are-rising-faster-than-gold-in-2026` has a second `<title>` inside the article body;
  - 37 articles have more than one `<h1>`.

## 3. Performance note (inferred, not measured field data)
The crawl reports median TTFB **1,875 ms** against 26 ms on 10-23, with 240 `PERF-SLOW-TTFB`. This is **mostly a measurement artefact**:
- **Local network:** the auditing machine's network is degraded today. Ping to 1.1.1.1 averages 68 ms; on 10-24 it was ~5 ms to connect to Cloudflare. Even Cloudflare cache HITs took 0.5–0.7 s from here.
- **Proxy change:** the 10-23 run hit the origin directly, before Cloudflare.

**Do not act on this number.** Check Search Console → Settings → Crawl stats → average response time, and CrUX/PageSpeed, once the PSI quota resets.

Two real lab findings:
- `/news` lab LCP is 8.3 s, while every other page is ≤3.4 s. Inspect its lead image.
- `/calculator/ppf` throws React hydration error #418 (text mismatch).

## 4. Why Google may still say "crawled – currently not indexed"
Technical blockers are now essentially gone: all 169 sitemap URLs are clean, metadata is in `<head>`, and there are no fake records. What remains is site-level quality and trust:

1. **Old evidence, not new.** Google's last full assessment saw the fictitious records, 114 thin data shells, seed articles, overclaiming trust copy and Googlebot-invisible metadata. Re-evaluation of a site-wide "not worth indexing" decision takes weeks of recrawls.
   - The sitemap went 247 → 169, and 165 old URLs are still reported 404 in GSC.
   - Expect movement in GSC only after the reasons' "Validation" re-runs.
2. **Data accuracy on the most-indexed product type (IPO).**
   - Priority Jewels (§2-B) shows wrong figures under "Official" badges.
   - Most IPO data is re-published from Groww, Investorgain and Chittorgarh (MAN-15, MAN-16). Google already has those numbers from the source sites, so these pages add little unique value.
3. **E-E-A-T for YMYL.**
   - One author, a developer, writes on IPO recommendations, credit cards and tax, with no credentials, no reviewer and no external citations or mentions of the site.
   - /about still contradicts itself (§2-C), and the contact email is hidden by Cloudflare (§2-A).
4. **Publishing pattern.**
   - Nothing was published between 19 Jul and 22 Oct.
   - Then about 20 articles (12 Hindi) appeared in 5 days, many on the same events, counting the Hindi versions (Jio Platforms IPO ×4, repo rate ×4).
   - Steady cadence and depth on fewer topics will help more than bursts.
5. **No authority signals.** The site does not appear for `site:gpaisa.in` or brand queries in the web search used for this review. An older gpaisa.in-titled page appears on a third-party host (`next-cms.climatetrace.org`, unverified, connection refused). That suggests scraped copies may exist.
