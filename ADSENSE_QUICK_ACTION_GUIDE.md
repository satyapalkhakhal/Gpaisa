## 📊 Content Quality Checklist

Use this for EVERY article you create or enhance:

### Before Publishing:
- [ ] **Word Count**: 800-1500 words minimum
- [ ] **Original Content**: Not copied, not pure AI
- [ ] **Human Review**: You've read and edited it
- [ ] **Value Addition**: Provides unique insights
- [ ] **Structure**: Clear H1, H2, H3 headings
- [ ] **Images**: At least 1 featured image
- [ ] **Internal Links**: 2-3 links to other articles
- [ ] **External Sources**: 1-2 citations
- [ ] **Meta Description**: 140-155 characters
- [ ] **SEO Keywords**: Natural keyword usage
- [ ] **Readability**: Easy to read and understand
- [ ] **Accuracy**: Facts verified
- [ ] **Engaging**: Interesting and useful
- [ ] **Complete**: Comprehensive coverage

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

1. ❌ **Don't resubmit to AdSense yet** - Wait 2-3 months
2. ❌ **Don't use only AI content** - Always add human insights
3. ❌ **Don't copy from other sites** - Write original content
4. ❌ **Don't publish thin content** - Minimum 800 words
5. ❌ **Don't auto-publish** - Review everything manually
6. ❌ **Don't buy traffic** - Build organic traffic only
7. ❌ **Don't have duplicate content** - Each article unique
8. ❌ **Don't ignore mobile users** - Test on mobile
